Tomato Egg Stir-fry

  • 2 tomatoes
  • 3 eggs
  • a pinch of salt
  • some cooking oil
  1. Cut tomatoes into pieces.
  2. Beat eggs and fry them.
  3. Add tomatoes and stir together.
