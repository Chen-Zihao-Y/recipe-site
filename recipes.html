番茄炒蛋 / Scrambled Eggs with Tomatoes

Ingredients

  • 2 tomatoes
  • 3 eggs
  • a pinch of salt
  • a little sugar
  • some cooking oil

Steps

  1. Cut tomatoes into pieces.
  2. Beat eggs and fry them in the pan.
  3. Add tomatoes, sugar and salt, stir and cook together.

清炒西兰花 / Stir-fried Broccoli

Ingredients

  • one head of broccoli
  • 2 cloves of garlic
  • a pinch of salt
  • some cooking oil

Steps

  1. Cut broccoli into small florets and wash them.
  2. Boil broccoli for 1 minute and drain the water.
  3. Fry garlic, add broccoli and salt, stir-fry for a short time.

酸辣土豆丝 / Sour and Spicy Shredded Potato

Ingredients

  • 2 potatoes
  • dry chili
  • vinegar
  • a pinch of salt
  • some cooking oil

Steps

  1. Peel potatoes and cut them into thin shreds.
  2. Wash potato shreds to remove extra starch.
  3. Fry chili, add potato shreds, vinegar and salt, stir-fry quickly.

黄瓜炒蛋 / Cucumber Egg Stir-fry

Ingredients

  • 1 cucumber
  • 3 eggs
  • a pinch of salt
  • some cooking oil

Steps

  1. Slice the cucumber.
  2. Beat eggs and fry them.
  3. Add cucumber slices and salt, stir and cook together.

清炒上海青 / Stir-fried Shanghai Bok Choy

Ingredients

  • 2 bunches of bok choy
  • 2 cloves of garlic
  • a pinch of salt
  • some cooking oil

Steps

  1. Wash the bok choy and drain water.
  2. Chop garlic and fry until fragrant.
  3. Put in bok choy and salt, stir-fry until soft.

香菇滑鸡 / Mushroom Chicken

Ingredients

  • 200g chicken thigh meat
  • 4 dried shiitake mushrooms
  • a little soy sauce
  • a pinch of salt
  • some cooking oil

Steps

  1. Soak mushrooms in warm water and cut chicken into pieces.
  2. Marinate chicken with soy sauce for 10 minutes.
  3. Fry mushrooms and chicken, add a little water and simmer until cooked.
